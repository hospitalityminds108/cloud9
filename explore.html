The Hills Beyond Cloud 9

Viewpoints, lakes, forts and ancient caves scattered across the Western Ghats — all within reach of the resort.

Closest to Cloud 9

Tiger Point

A cliffside viewpoint looking straight into the valley — one of the closest lookouts to the resort, especially striking at sunrise and in the monsoon mist.

Get Directions
Short Drive Away

Khandala

Lonavala's twin hill town, known for its own set of viewpoints, the ghat road views and a handful of well-known local eateries.

Get Directions
A Longer Drive

Rajmachi

A twin-fort trekking destination deep in the Sahyadris, popular with monsoon trekkers for its waterfalls and sweeping valley views.

Get Directions
Popular Monsoon Stop

Bhushi Dam

One of the region's most-visited monsoon spots — cascading spillway water that draws crowds through July and August.

Get Directions
Half-Day Trip

Karla Caves

Rock-cut Buddhist caves dating back over two thousand years, home to one of India's largest and best-preserved chaitya halls.

Get Directions
Near Karla Caves

Bhaja Caves

A quieter, older set of rock-cut caves close to Karla — known for their carved stupas and monastic cells.

Get Directions
Popular Day Trek

Lohagad Fort

An accessible hill fort with a well-known narrow ridge approach, popular with first-time trekkers and monsoon day-trippers alike.

Get Directions
A Scenic Drive

Pawna Lake

A large reservoir ringed by hills, known for lakeside camping and calm, still-water views — a good half-day detour from the resort.

Get Directions
Along the Same Road

Amby Valley

A large planned township along Amby Valley Road, on the same route as Cloud 9 — known for its scale and its own set of scenic drives.
