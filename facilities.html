Good to Know

Facilities — Frequently Asked

What facilities does Cloud 9 Hills Resort offer?

A swimming pool, an on-site spa, indoor and outdoor games, forest trekking, cycling routes, an on-site temple, a bonfire area and a sunset viewpoint.

Is trekking available at Cloud 9 Hills Resort?

Yes. Forest trails around the resort lead through the Western Ghats, past waterfall and valley views typical of the Sahyadris. Ask our team for current routes and arrangements.

Can guests go cycling near Cloud 9 Hills Resort?

Yes. The hill roads around the resort make for scenic cycling, especially early morning or at sunset. Ask our team for routes and equipment arrangements.

Is the swimming pool open to all guests?

Yes, the on-site swimming pool is open to everyone staying at the resort.
