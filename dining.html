A Table in the Sky

Dining That
Floats Above the World

Every meal at Cloud 9 is a quiet conversation with the mountains—fresh, honest, and deeply rooted in this land.

Our Philosophy

From the Hills to Your Plate

We believe the best food is grown where you live. That’s why our menus change with the season, using produce from local farmers and herbs from our own kitchen garden. We cook with the Western Ghats in mind—light, aromatic, and never overcomplicated.
