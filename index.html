The Cloud 9 Experience

A Different Kind of Escape

Cloud 9 Hills Resort sit 1800ft above khandala and 3000ft above sea level , where forested slopes meet drifting monsoon cloud. It's a peaceful luxury retreat built for slow mornings, long views and quiet evenings by the fire — a place designed around the rhythm of the Western Ghats rather than against it.

An Experience for Every Moment

Rooftop Restaurant

7 Heaven Restaurant

Our all-day dining restaurant, serving breakfast through dinner with a menu spanning Indian, Punjabi, Chinese and South Indian favourites — all with a view of the hills.

  • All-Day Dining

Candle Light Dinner

A private table for two, set with candles and warm lighting as the valley slips into evening. Curated vegetarian menu, ideal for anniversaries, proposals and slow, unhurried celebrations.

  • For Two

Bonfire Dining
