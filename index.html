The Cloud 9 Experience

A Different Kind
of Escape

Cloud 9 Hills Resort sits high above Lonavala, where forested slopes meet drifting monsoon cloud. It's a peaceful luxury retreat built for slow mornings, long views and quiet evenings by the fire — a place designed around the rhythm of the Western Ghats rather than against it.
