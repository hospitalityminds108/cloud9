The Wedding Story

Where the hills become family

Cloud 9 sits above Lonavala, far enough from the city to feel like a destination and close enough for everyone to make it. Mist in the morning, gold light by evening — the landscape does half the decorating.

Our team plans the days with you: rituals, décor partners, menus and rooms, so the only thing you carry is the moment.

Celebrate Every Ritual

Every ceremony, its own setting

Haldi

A morning of marigold and laughter

Poolside or on the lawn, in the soft light of the hills — bright drapes, brass urlis and marigold everywhere, with family close enough to join in.

  • Poolside or lawn
  • Marigold décor
  • Morning light

Mehndi

Henna, music and long afternoons

Floral lounges and low seating for the bride, her artists and everyone who wants to watch — an easy afternoon that drifts into the evening's sangeet.

  • Floral lounges
  • Afternoon to evening
  • Sangeet to follow
