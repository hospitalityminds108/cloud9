What you can expect

Everything that makes
the day feel effortless.

Weddings at Cloud 9 are designed as partnerships. We hold the setting, the logistics and the hospitality — you bring the vision.

01

Dedicated wedding coordinator

A single point of contact from your first enquiry through to the farewell morning.

02

Flexible vendor policy

Bring your own decorator, caterer, DJ or photographer — or let us suggest trusted partners.

03

In-house catering option

Menus designed around your celebration — from multi-course dinners to relaxed brunches.

04

On-site guest stay

Villas, suites and cottages for your wedding party — no long drives, no coordination gaps.

05

Indoor & outdoor options

An open lawn for the ceremony and an indoor hall as a monsoon-proof fallback.

06

Ample parking & access

Convenient access for arriving guests, vendors and vehicles — the logistics handled quietly in the background.
