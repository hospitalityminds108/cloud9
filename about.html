About Cloud 9

A Hillside Escape
Above the Ordinary

Set high above Lonavala in the folds of the Western Ghats — a resort shaped by silence, mist and slow mornings.

Our Philosophy

Built Around the
Rhythm of the Hills

Cloud 9 was conceived as a private escape rather than a conventional hotel — a place where the pace of the mountains sets the pace of your stay.

Every villa, cottage and shared space is oriented toward the valley, making the landscape an essential part of the experience.
